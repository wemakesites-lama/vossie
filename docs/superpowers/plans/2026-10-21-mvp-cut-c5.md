## Task 23: a critical-flow suite instead of the whole e2e suite on Supabase

**What's deferred:** porting all ~40 specs (about 1,300 device tests and the seven write projects) to Supabase. That
means a session helper behind about 135 sign-in call sites, raising local Auth's limits, checking every persona against
the message limits, and two full resets. It's days of work, and most of it re-proves pages that render the same on both
backends: layout, axe, motion, CLS and responsive don't depend on where the data comes from. Those stay covered by
the sample-mode suite on main.

**What's added:** one spec file, `tests/e2e-supabase/mvp.spec.ts`, in the existing Supabase config, after
`supabase db reset`. Each test gets a fresh address through the existing helpers (`newAddress`, `latestCode` from
Mailpit, `removeE2eAccounts` afterwards), so no persona hits the message limits. The first admin comes from
`private.make_first_admin`, as on the live project (runbook B11). Two projects (390 and 1440), one worker, as now.

| # | Flow | Checks on Supabase |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Public reads** | Home, browse (a search and a campus filter), a listing, a storefront and a photo from `/photos/[id]` all show the seed. A Hub post and a Looking For request open. |
| 2 | **Becoming a seller** | A new student applies for a shop. An admin approves it (`sellers` expires). The seller publishes a listing with one photo, and it's on browse and its own page. |
| 3 | **Messaging** | A second student messages about that listing. The seller sees it in the inbox, with the header's count, and replies. The buyer sees the reply and a notification. |
| 4 | **Report and hide** | The buyer reports the listing. The admin hides it from the report, and it's gone from browse (`listings`). Show again brings it back, and the seller is told. |
| 5 | **Looking For** | A student posts a request (`requests`). The approved shop sends an offer. The asker sees it, and an offer doesn't count as an enquiry. |
| 6 | **Follow and alert** | A student follows the shop and saves a search. The seller publishes a matching listing. The student gets both notifications, once. |
| 7 | **Hub** | The admin publishes an event (`hub`). A student RSVPs, and the RSVP is in their data download by title, with no ids. |
| 8 | **Roles** | The admin gives the mentor role by exact email. The mentor sees the dashboard. A student gets the 404 view on `/admin`. Removing the last admin is refused. |
| 9 | **Own shop** | The admin, now a seller, can't approve or pin their own shop: one line instead of the controls. |
| 10 | **Numbers** | `/admin/numbers` loads for the admin, and a campus with 1 to 4 shows "Hidden" (`protectCounts`). |
| 11 | **Deletion** | The seller deletes their account. The shop, listings, follows and their requests are gone, and the audit log says "Deleted account". |
| 12 | **Crons** | `/api/cron/photos` and `/api/cron/badges` answer 401 without `CRON_SECRET` and 200 with it. The config's `webServer.env` gains a random `CRON_SECRET`, as it does the HMAC key. |

Flows 2 to 11 share state, so they run in order (`test.describe.configure({ mode: "serial" })`). Flows 1 and 12 stand
alone. Whatever fails is a bug in its module, fixed there with a contract test first, never by changing the assertion
(Task 23's rule, kept).

**Rough size:** one spec and a few helper functions, against Task 23's helper rewrites and every spec's
`onSupabaseE2E()` variants.
