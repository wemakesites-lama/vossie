How should people choose their campus?

In all three, picking a campus puts that campus first everywhere on the site, and "All campuses" stays one tap away, so nobody is ever locked to one campus. The header now uses the pennant you picked.

Campus names are the 12 on eduvos.com plus Eduvos Online. Blue boxes are where campus photos would go. My recommendation is A: it borrows Brigade's "Shop by brand" section and makes it slowly cycle through campuses, so you still get your animated carousel, but cleaner.

A

Campus switcher + "Shop by campus" list

A campus pill in the header opens a picker (a bottom sheet on phones) with "All campuses" pinned on top and a search box. On the home page, campus names sit left and right of one photo, Brigade-style; the highlight moves to the next campus every few seconds and the photo swaps, pausing when you hover or tap.

Desktop: home page section
Phone: switcher sheet
For: cleanest and closest to Brigade; the same pill works on every page; type-led, so it stays light on mobile data.Against: needs one photo per campus; the hover swap is a desktop moment (phones get a tap-to-swap list).
B

Campus card carousel

Your original idea: a swipeable row of campus cards under the hero, starting with "All campuses", drifting slowly until someone touches it. Tapping a card opens that campus's home. The same header pill handles switching later.

Desktop
Phone
For: the most visual and playful; every campus gets its own card.Against: needs 12 strong campus photos; auto-moving rows are often skipped; heavier on data.
C

Ask on first visit

The first time someone arrives, a full-screen "Where do you study?" picker appears before the home page. It's remembered after that, and the header pill changes it.

Desktop
Phone
For: every page is tailored from the first second; no campus photos needed.Against: an extra step before anyone sees a listing, which costs visitors; awkward for people who just want to browse everywhere.
