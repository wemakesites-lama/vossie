The home page, top to bottom

Brigade's home page works because it's short and every section has one job. This keeps that rhythm with your two decisions built in (the pennant and the campus carousel). Listing names are generic examples, not the demo's items. Photos, copy and categories are still placeholders.

1
Announcement barOne useful line at a time: launches, a safety tip, a market day. Phone: wraps to two lines.
2
HeaderPennant crest, campus pill, search, account, Saved. Quick links on desktop only. Search opens a full-screen panel with recent and trending searches on your campus. Phone: menu and search left, account and Saved right.
3
Hero sliderBrigade's trick: the headline stays, one word changes (Textbooks, Tech, Services), and each slide opens its own category. Thumbnails plus a pause button. Phone: photo on top, navy panel below.
4
Shop your campusYour pick. Drifts slowly until touched; tapping a campus opens the site with that campus first.
5
Shop by categoryPhoto tiles with the cut-out arrow corner. The category list is the next decision. Phone: swipe row with a progress bar.
6
Just listedThe newest listings on your campus (or all), so the page always shows what's live today. Phone: two-column grid.
7
Featured todayThe fair daily draw of sellers the demo already does, with "How we pick". Puts students' faces on the page.
8
CollectionsBrigade's hover list, curated by the Hub for the time of year: back to campus, exam season, moving into res, skills for hire. Phone: rows with a thumbnail.
9
How it worksBuying and selling in three steps each, with the safety rules underneath. Builds trust for first-timers.
10
Incubation Hub bannerBrigade's "About us" slot: who's behind Vossie, plus Hub events and office hours.
11
FooterSign-up for campus updates, help, safety, POPIA privacy, seller guidelines.

Which extras should we add to make it more useful?

Tick as many as you like. Each one builds on something the demo already does. The ones marked Suggested give the most value for the least clutter.

1

Your activity strip Suggested

Signed-in only, right under the header: "2 new messages", "1 sale waiting for you to confirm", "A saved item is back". Turns the home page into a dashboard for regulars.

2

Budget picks Suggested

"Under R50", "Under R100", "Under R250" quick buttons under the categories. Students shop by budget first.

3

Free and swaps row Suggested

Giveaways and swap-only listings in one row: old textbooks, res furniture at the end of the year, skill swaps. Builds on the demo's swap feature.

4

Recently viewed

A row of what you looked at last time, for returning visitors. Stored on the device, no sign-in needed.

5

Looking For band

"Can't find it? Post what you need" and let sellers come to you. The demo has this as a placeholder; it would need building.

6

What's on at the Hub

The next two or three Hub events and office hours as small cards, with RSVP. Builds on the demo's Hub Growth area.

7

Trust strip near the top

Three small reassurances under the hero: Verified sellers, Approved pickup points, Report anything. Helps first-time visitors.

8

Add to home screen

A gentle prompt to install Vossie like an app: opens faster, uses less data, works offline. The demo already supports this; it just isn't promoted.
